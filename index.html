About

Hi, I’m Mahmud.

I’m a PhD student in the Adaptive Robotics Lab at Colorado State University, advised by Dr. Jianguo Zhao. I completed my M.S. at CSU and my B.Sc. in Mechanical Engineering at BUET.

My research focuses on tensegrity robots — structures of rigid struts held in balance by elastic cables — and how their morphology can adapt for locomotion, manipulation and cooperation. I currently work on a tensegrity robotic fish and a modular tensegrity worm, both supported by NSF. Before grad school I modeled geothermal systems for an EU-funded project in the UK.
